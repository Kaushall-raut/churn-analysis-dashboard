# 📊 Churn Analysis Dashboard for Telecom Company

A professional **Power BI Dashboard** built to analyze customer churn behavior in a telecom company.  
This dashboard provides insights into customer demographics, billing behavior, internet services, payment methods, and customer retention patterns.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the major challenges in the telecom industry.  
This dashboard helps businesses understand:

- Customer churn trends
- Revenue and charges
- Customer demographics
- Service usage behavior
- Payment preferences
- Retention patterns

The project is designed using **Power BI** with interactive charts, KPIs, and filters for better business decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

### 🔹 Churn Analysis
- Approximately **26.54%** customers have churned.
- Around **73.46%** customers are retained.

### 🔹 Internet Service Analysis
- Fiber optic users are highest in number.
- DSL users are moderate.
- Customers without internet service are lowest.

### 🔹 Payment Method Analysis
- Electronic check is the most commonly used payment method.
- Mailed checks, bank transfers, and credit cards are also widely used.

### 🔹 Paperless Billing
- Most customers prefer paperless billing services.

### 🔹 Demographic Insights
- Male and female customer distribution is almost equal.
- Senior citizens represent a smaller percentage of customers.

### 🔹 Tenure Analysis
- Customers with longer tenure tend to stay longer with the company.
